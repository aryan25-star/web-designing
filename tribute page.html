Dwayne Johnson "The Rock"

About Him

Dwayne Johnson was born in 1972. He is a very famous person who started as a football player and then became a wrestler. Now he is one of the best actors in the world.Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson is a global icon whose journey from having only seven dollars in his pocket to becoming a multi-billion-dollar brand is a masterclass in resilience. Born into a legendary wrestling lineage, he first pursued professional football before finding his true calling in the WWE. As "The Most Electrifying Man in Sports Entertainment," his charismatic promos and rivalries with legends like Stone Cold Steve Austin defined the "Attitude Era," earning him ten world championships and a permanent place in wrestling history. His transition to Hollywood shattered the stereotype of the "wrestler-turned-actor." Starting with The Mummy Returns, he evolved into the world’s most bankable movie star, anchoring massive franchises like Fast & Furious, Jumanji, and Moana. Beyond the screen, Johnson is a formidable mogul, owning the UFL, Seven Bucks Productions, and the record-breaking Teremana Tequila. Now a board member of TKO Group Holdings, he continues to bridge the worlds of corporate business and high-octane entertainment. Whether he is performing as the "Final Boss" in the ring or leading a blockbuster film, his philosophy remains the same: "Be humble, be hungry, and always be the hardest worker in the room."
